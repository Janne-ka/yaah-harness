NOTICE RW-2031-074 — Quarterly Emissions Reporting Format
Issuing body: Fictional Environmental Reporting Board (FERB)
Effective: 2032-02-01

The quarterly emissions return moves from a spreadsheet upload to a structured
form with three additional fields (source category, measurement method,
uncertainty band). Historical data need not be restated.

Impact note: reporting team maps existing data to the new fields before Q1 close.
