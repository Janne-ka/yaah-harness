NOTICE RW-2031-014 — Packaging Legibility Amendment
Issuing body: Fictional Consumer Goods Bureau (FCGB)
Effective: 2031-09-01

Minimum font size for net-quantity statements on retail packaging rises
from 1.6mm to 2.0mm. A six-month sell-through window applies to stock
already printed. No change to placement or wording rules.

Impact note: minor artwork refresh at next print cycle.
