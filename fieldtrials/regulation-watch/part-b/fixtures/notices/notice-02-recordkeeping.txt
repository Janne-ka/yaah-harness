NOTICE RW-2031-021 — Retention Schedule Clarification
Issuing body: Fictional Records & Archives Authority (FRAA)
Effective: 2031-10-15

Clarifies that shipment manifests fall under the existing 7-year retention
class, not the 3-year general-correspondence class. No new obligation is
created; this resolves an ambiguity flagged in prior guidance.

Impact note: update the internal retention matrix wording only.
